About Nuggercise

Welcome to Nuggercise! ⚡

Big fitness goals, broken down into bite-sized habits. Nuggercise helps you build consistency by logging your daily active moments—your fitness "nuggets"—and turning them into a rewarding streak.

How to use it in 3 steps:

•
Log a Nugget: Did a quick stretch, a short walk, or a heavy workout? Tap the "+" button to log it.
•
Unlock Rewards: Every log instantly reveals a random reward tier—Common, Energetic, or Legendary—complete with arcade audio and celebration highlights!
•
Keep it Alive: Log at least one fitness nugget every day to build your streak grid and watch your physical progress compound over time.
